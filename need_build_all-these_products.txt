Surya, here’s a concise but deep technical report on Palantir’s products—**what each platform is**, **how it works**, **key features**, and **how they interlock as one ecosystem**.

## Executive summary (what to remember)

* **Palantir has four principal software platforms:**
  **AIP** (Artificial Intelligence Platform), **Foundry** (data operations & applications), **Gotham** (defense/intel decision platform), and **Apollo** (mission‑critical software delivery). This four‑stack is explicitly confirmed in Palantir’s latest 10‑K. ([Palantir Investors][1])
* **Ecosystem view:** Apollo (delivery) → Foundry (data & ontology) ↔ AIP (LLM agents, logic, evals) ↔ Gotham (operational decision‑making for defense). Palantir calls this integrated architecture an **AI Mesh**; **AIP Now** provides pre‑built, production workflows on top. ([Palantir][2])
* **Differentiator:** the **Ontology**—a governed object/graph layer that maps real‑world entities and actions, natively powering apps, functions, and LLM agents. ([Palantir][3])

---

## Product 1 — Palantir AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform)

**What it is:** AIP connects LLMs and other AI models to enterprise data and operations, with tooling for **agents**, **no‑code LLM logic**, **evals/observability**, and **governed integration** across the platform. ([Palantir][4])

**Core builder & user capabilities**

* **Agent Studio:** build interactive, tool‑using agents grounded in the Ontology and documents; deploy inside Palantir apps or via SDKs. ([Palantir][5])
* **AIP Logic (no‑code):** create LLM‑backed functions, engineer prompts, test, automate, and release—without writing service glue. ([Palantir][6])
* **AIP Evals & Observability:** test prompts/functions, maintain quality; view **run history (7‑day retention)**, distributed **trace** timelines, logs, token usage, and performance. ([Palantir][7])
* **AIP Assist:** context‑aware, in‑platform help that respects data governance. ([Palantir][8])
* **Document Intelligence:** point‑and‑click document extraction (optionally with OCR + VLM preprocessing), benchmark quality/speed/token cost, and **one‑click deploy** as a Python transform. ([Palantir][9])
* **Threads:** enterprise chat workspace to work with documents and agents—no extra setup beyond enabling AIP workflows. ([Palantir][10])
* **Model Catalog:** catalog of Palantir‑provided LLMs (currently **LLMs only**, not custom ML models). ([Palantir][11])
* **Dev toolchain & openness:** **Ontology SDK** (TypeScript, Python, Java), and **Palantir MCP** to connect external AI IDEs/agents. ([Palantir][8])
* **Security & governance:** AIP inherits Foundry’s access controls, encryption, audit & lineage; Palantir publishes FAQs via its trust portal. ([Palantir][12])

**Where AIP runs / how it’s packaged**

* Part of Palantir’s **AI Mesh** with Foundry and Apollo; delivered as a governed capability across the platform and featured in **AIP Now** (pre‑built, production workflows). ([Palantir][2])

---

## Product 2 — Palantir Foundry

**What it is:** A data operations platform whose **Ontology** models your world (objects, links, actions, interfaces) and powers both analytics and operational applications. ([Palantir][3])

**Key platform components**

* **Data Integration:** connect on‑prem/cloud systems; build pipelines from raw → clean outputs with **Pipeline Builder** (versioning, branches, transforms, large‑pipeline management). ([Palantir][13])
* **Ontology:** define **object types**, **link types**, and **action types**; map properties to backing data; the Ontology becomes the semantic/operational backbone. ([Palantir][14])
* **Functions & FOO:** server‑side business logic callable in apps; first‑class bindings to Ontology; **TypeScript/Python** supported. ([Palantir][15])
* **Applications:**

  * **Workshop** (low‑code): build interactive, mobile‑ready operational apps on the Ontology; uses **Actions** for write‑back and Functions for business logic. ([Palantir][16])
  * **Code Workspaces:** managed JupyterLab/RStudio/VS Code with platform security & governance; train/publish models; guidance on PySpark usage. ([Palantir][17])
* **Security & governance:** platform‑wide fine‑grained access control; **Checkpoints** (capture user justifications for sensitive interactions) for accountability. ([Palantir][18])
* **Deployment models:** **Foundry SaaS** and **Foundry On‑Premises**; Palantir documents hardening and zero‑trust controls for self‑hosted installs. ([Palantir][19])
* **Data connection & agents:** step‑by‑step guidance for networking/agents to connect external systems. ([Palantir][20])

---

## Product 3 — Palantir Gotham

**What it is:** An operational decision platform for defense/intelligence—live **common operating picture**, AI‑assisted mission planning, simulation, and effects assessment, across echelons and domains. 

**Key capabilities (Defense/IC focus)**

* **Live COP & sensor fusion:** integrate historical + streaming sensor data; surface AI insights in near real time. 
* **Mission planning & COA simulation:** structured planning, what‑ifs, and time‑driven constraints; capture decisions for after‑action learning. 
* **Open & interoperable:** APIs, 3rd‑party AI/model integration; **cloud or on‑prem** deployment; designed to be operational within weeks. 
* **Developer & security surface:** REST **Gotham API** (OAuth2), admin controls for 3rd‑party app registration, and documented security posture for handling sensitive data (PII/PHI/CUI, HIPAA/GDPR/ITAR alignment). ([Palantir][21])

> **Gotham ↔ Foundry interop.** You can **type‑map** Foundry Ontology objects to Gotham types and keep them synced; enable cross‑app **drag‑and‑drop enrichment** via a synonym service, so apps can pass object context between Foundry and Gotham seamlessly. ([Palantir][22])

---

## Product 4 — Palantir Apollo

**What it is:** **Mission‑critical software delivery & day‑2 ops**. Apollo upgrades, monitors, secures, and remediates software across **heterogeneous** and even **disconnected/classified** environments. It underpins how Palantir runs its own platforms anywhere. ([Palantir][23])

**How it works**

* **Hub‑and‑Spoke architecture:** Spokes are typically **Kubernetes clusters**; connect local/prod clusters; works across public cloud, on‑prem, air‑gapped. ([Palantir][24])
* **Agents & plans:** **Apollo Agents** run in Spokes and execute “Plans” issued by the Hub; **outbound‑only** encrypted comms from environment to Hub (operator‑controlled allowlists). ([Palantir][25])
* **Safety controls:** **Constraints** gate when plans can run; orchestration & release promotion; RBAC/approvals for **change management**. ([Palantir][26])
* **Concepts:** **Products/Releases/Versions** model deployable software; **Modules** bundle entities for install/update across environments. ([Palantir][27])

## How the products work together (the ecosystem)

1. **Delivery layer — Apollo**
   Apollo continuously deploys and operates Palantir software (and your own code) across clouds, on‑prem, and classified/edge sites. This gives the same **SaaS‑style** cadence and safety everywhere. ([Palantir][23])

2. **Data & operations layer — Foundry**
   Your data is ingested and transformed in **Pipeline Builder**; the **Ontology** models entities/relationships/actions; **Workshop** and **Functions** turn this into operational apps and write‑back workflows. ([Palantir][13])

3. **AI layer — AIP**
   Builders attach **AIP Logic** and **Agent Studio** to the Ontology to create LLM‑powered functions and agents; **Evals/Observability** enforce quality; **Threads** and **Assist** let end‑users safely use AI in their daily work. ([Palantir][6])

4. **Defense/intel operations (optional) — Gotham**
   For missions, Gotham consumes/synchronizes Ontology objects from Foundry (via **type mapping**) and fuses operational feeds for live decision‑making, planning, and simulation. ([Palantir][22])

5. **Pre‑built apps — AIP Now**
   Ready‑made, production workflows (e.g., cost 360, customer service, etc.) demonstrate patterns and accelerate time‑to‑value atop the AI Mesh. ([Palantir][2])

## Example end‑to‑end pattern (how the ecosystem is used)

> **Scenario:** Parts shortage risk → mitigation actions with AI

1. **Ingest/prepare data** (ERP, suppliers, logistics) in **Pipeline Builder**; produce clean outputs. ([Palantir][13])
2. **Model the world** (suppliers, plants, SKUs, POs, lanes) in the **Ontology**; define **Actions** like *Reallocate inventory* and *Expedite shipment*. ([Palantir][3])
3. **Build an app** in **Workshop** for planners to see risks and trigger Actions; **Functions** compute risk scores. ([Palantir][16])
4. **Add AI** with **AIP Logic** and an **Agent** that summarizes risk, proposes options, and executes Actions under governance; monitor with **AIP Observability**. ([Palantir][6])
5. **Operate everywhere** via **Apollo** (prod clusters, edge sites); controlled, auditable rollouts with constraints and approvals. ([Palantir][24])

## Trade‑offs, risks, and realities

* **Ontology first:** You get compounding value, but it requires upfront modeling and disciplined data integration. (This is Palantir’s core design choice.) ([Palantir][3])
* **Governance overhead:** Strong controls (e.g., **Checkpoints**) add friction by design; they’re essential for regulated ops. ([Palantir][35])
* **LLM ops**: You must budget for prompt evals, token usage, and runtime monitoring (AIP provides primitives, but you still own the choices). ([Palantir][7])
* **Hybrid/air‑gapped deployments:** Powerful via Apollo, but your infra/network teams need to follow Palantir’s hardening and egress allowlisting guidance. ([Palantir][25])

## Glossary (brief)

* **Ontology:** objects/links/actions/interfaces that map data to your operational reality. ([Palantir][40])
* **Actions / FOO:** governed writes & logic over Ontology objects. ([Palantir][41])
* **AIP Agent / Logic / Evals / Observability:** agentic workflows, no‑code LLM functions, prompt/testing suites, and runtime tracing/logging. ([Palantir][5])
* **Apollo Hub/Spoke/Agent/Constraints:** control plane, target clusters, execution workers, and safety gates for releases. ([Palantir][24])

* **AIP** turns your Ontology into safe, observable agentic automation.
* **Foundry** is the substrate that makes AI and apps operational and governed.

* **Apollo** is how all of this reliably ships and runs anywhere.
