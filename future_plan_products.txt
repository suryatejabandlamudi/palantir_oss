ERP — Enterprise Resource Planning

Example: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (D365 BC)

What it does

The ERP is the financial + operational backbone of the company.

Core functions

GL (General Ledger) — accounting

AP/AR (Accounts Payable/Receivable) — bills & customer invoices

Inventory & MRP (Material Requirements Planning)

POs (Purchase Orders)

Sales Orders

Fixed Assets

Basic supply chain + manufacturing

Who uses it

Finance, supply chain, operations, accounting.

Why D365 BC

Good for mid-market companies

pricing

Everything from lead → deal → contract is in one place.
